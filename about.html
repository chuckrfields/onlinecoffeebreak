About the podcast

Celebrities, musicians, actors, movies, and science.

Welcome to “Your Online Coffee Break,” episodes that inspire, educate, and entertain with host Chuck Fields. The show interviewed go-getters around, and above, the world on sci-fi, new music, space, science, entertainment, faith, and more, in bite-sized episodes.

Guests included Zoe Perry from Young Sheldon, Queen’s Brian May, Earth, Wind & Fire’s Verdine White and Ralph Johnson, Angela Cartwright, the Go-Go’s, The B-52’s, Air Supply, Foghat, Collective Soul, astronauts, authors, and directors.

Chuck Fields

Software innovator, award-winning marketer, and astronomy buff. Host of Your Online Coffee Break.

More than 20 years in application development, business analysis, marketing, and project leadership. He fell in love with astronomy young, moved into astrophotography, and had a photo published internationally at 15. He started a web and software company in 2006. The podcast came from the idea that sometimes you need to step back from the busyness of life in order to see clearly.
